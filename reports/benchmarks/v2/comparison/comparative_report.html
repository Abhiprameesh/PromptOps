🎯 Best Category Domain Routing: mistral:latest
Highest category classification accuracy (70.00%) and macro F1 (0.6894), correctly mapping diverse architectural issues to their exact domain. Recommended when issues are triaged to department queues (Security, UI, Backend, Database) where priority is manually adjusted later.
⚖️ Strategic Trade-Off & Deployment Architecture
Mistral 7B delivers superior category discrimination (70.00% vs 48.00% for Gemma) with top F1 scores in backend, database, and UI. However, Gemma 3 4B achieves superior composite accuracy (32.00% vs 24.00%) due to better priority triage calibration. For routing/triage where category routing is paramount, Mistral 7B is the recommended choice. For automated end-to-end SLA triage where priority and summary correctness are required, Gemma 3 4B is the recommended choice.
